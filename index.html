INTRODUCTION

About me

I am a QA Engineer who validates requirements, finds defects before they reach users and works with developers to get them fixed. At Point Pi I run functional, regression, smoke, acceptance and exploratory tests and design test cases.

I also have a background in Java, Oracle PL/SQL and HubSpot CRM, which helps me understand how systems and data connect. I am honest, hardworking and a quick learner who wants to grow with teams that value quality.

I believe quality assurance is about improving the reliability and user experience of a product, not only finding bugs.
